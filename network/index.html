What a network makes possible

A bot in one server only knows that server. Billy sees the same people move between fan games, and that changes what he can tell you.

Reputation, before the trouble

Sweep who you already have

One announcement, every server

Run several fan-game servers? Say it once and Billy posts it in all of them.

Knowledge that stays current

Billy follows the channels each community approves and keeps its answers specific to that server.

What the network never does

A reputation check reports numbers. Billy never passes on another server’s name, its moderators, or the reasons they wrote — those are someone else’s private staff decisions and not his to hand out.

A community that changed its mind and lifted a ban does not leave it on somebody’s record. It is a reason to look more closely, never a reason to punish on its own, and Billy never acts on it by himself.

Any server can stop contributing with /ultimate sharing — free or paid, no questions.
