Questions

What happens when the free answers run out?

Billy says so once, then stays quiet until midnight UTC. Moderation, tickets, protection and every other feature keep working as normal. Only the AI answers pause.

Is it per server or per account?

Per server. A plan applies to the server you buy it for, and answers are counted per server, so one busy community can never use up another's.

Can I cancel?

Any time. The server goes back to Free at the end of the period, and nothing you set up, taught Billy, or logged is deleted.

Does Billy stop working without a plan?

No. Free is a real plan, not a trial. Most servers never need more.

How can I pay?

Google Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay or any major card. Run /subscriptions buy in your server and Billy gives you a secure checkout page hosted by our payment provider. This site never sees, handles or stores card details.

How do I turn it on after paying?

You don't have to. Billy is told the moment the payment goes through and switches the plan on by himself, and he checks again every hour in case he was restarting. Run /subscriptions view to see what your server's plan includes.
