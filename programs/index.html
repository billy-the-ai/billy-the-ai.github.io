How applying works

  1. Fill in the form. A few questions about your experience and the community you help run. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Billy files it. Your application goes to the team's review channel and is checked against your Discord account.
  3. A person reads it. Not the bot. Someone on the team decides, and the decision is written down with a reason.
  4. You hear back either way. Including if the answer is no, and why.

Being honest: places are limited and most applications are declined, usually because the timing is wrong rather than the person. Applying doesn't change how Billy treats you or your server, and you can apply again later.

Not just for Billy. Every server with Billy can run these, with its own team roles, staff channels, Apply buttons and points. Set them up with a command or from the Control Room.

Helper Points

The members who end up as great moderators are usually already answering questions. Billy notices it on his own. Nobody has to clip, react or type a command.

  1. A member asks a question
  2. Someone at or above the level your admins chose answers it
  3. The person who asked says thanks, or that it worked
  4. Staff approve the picture Billy posts, or Billy gives the point automatically

  • Review or automatic
