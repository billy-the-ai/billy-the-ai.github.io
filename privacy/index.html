Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026 · Last updated August 6, 2026

This Policy explains what information Billy the AI processes, why it is needed, when it is shared, and the choices available to Discord users and server administrators.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Billy the AI Discord application, website, support server, and optional premium services. Discord separately controls information collected through its platform and payment systems; its own privacy policy also applies.

2. Information Billy may process

Discord account and server information

  • User, server, channel, role, message, command, and interaction IDs;
  • usernames, display names, avatars, roles, join dates, and server membership;
  • server names, channel names and topics, role configuration, and permissions; and
  • subscription entitlement and SKU identifiers, without full payment-card details.

Content you or a server submits

  • Messages directed to Billy or posted in channels configured for support, safety monitoring, or approved knowledge;
  • ticket, report, appeal, bug report, feedback, and form-submission content;
  • images, videos, files, and links submitted for evidence or analysis; and
  • staff-created notes, warnings, moderation reasons, and case records.

Activity and technical information

  • Message counts, XP, levels, ranks, streaks, invites, and helpful-activity signals;
  • time spent connected to voice channels, but not the audio of voice conversations;
  • command usage, response time, error logs, security events, and abuse-prevention signals; and
  • basic website request data needed for security and reliable delivery.

Information Billy does not intentionally collect

  • the audio of voice conversations;
  • full payment-card or bank-account numbers;
  • passwords, Discord tokens, or private keys; or
  • messages from unrelated private channels that Billy cannot access or was not configured to process.

3. How information is used

Billy processes information to:

  • answer questions using approved, server-specific knowledge;
  • provide commands, tickets, reports, moderation records, levels, and analytics;
  • understand conversation context and remember useful information when enabled;
  • detect spam, abuse, prompt injection, security threats, and service failures;
  • provide support, investigate reports, and improve accuracy and performance;
  • verify premium access and deliver purchased features; and
  • comply with law and enforce the Terms of Service.

4. Server knowledge and AI processing

Server administrators decide which channels Billy may use as approved knowledge sources and which channels may use support or safety features. Billy may create searchable representations, summaries, and freshness records from that content. Server knowledge is kept logically separated and is not used to answer questions in unrelated servers.

Content may be sent to a configured AI service provider or processed by a locally hosted model to generate a requested answer or analysis. Only the information reasonably needed for that feature should be included. Billy does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

5. Legal reasons for processing

Depending on where you live, Billy relies on performance of a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. Server administrators are responsible for having an appropriate basis to configure Billy for their community.

6. When information is shared

Information may be shared only as needed with:

  • Discord, because Billy operates through Discord's platform;
  • hosting, database, logging, file-storage, and AI service providers supporting Billy;
  • authorized staff in the relevant server for tickets, reports, or moderation records;
  • payment providers for subscription entitlements and billing support; and
  • authorities or affected parties when required by law or necessary to prevent serious harm.

Billy does not sell or rent personal information.

7. Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the feature, security, dispute resolution, or legal requirements. Approved knowledge remains until an administrator removes or replaces it, the server requests deletion, or Billy's retention process removes it. Ticket and moderation records may be retained longer for accountability. Temporary files and backups may take additional time to expire securely.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. A server administrator can also remove knowledge sources, clear server data, change Billy's permissions, or remove the bot. Requests may require verification of your Discord identity and authority over the server. Removing Billy stops new collection from that server but does not automatically erase records that must be retained for security, disputes, or legal obligations.

9. Security

Billy uses reasonable technical and organizational measures such as permission checks, server separation, limited access, secret management, logging, rate limits, and encrypted network connections. No online service is perfectly secure, so users should never submit passwords, bot tokens, payment details, government identifiers, or unnecessary sensitive personal information to Billy.

10. Children and teens

Billy is not intended for anyone below Discord's minimum required age. If we learn that information was collected from someone who could not legally use Discord, we will take reasonable steps to remove it. Parents or guardians may contact the Billy team about a privacy concern involving a minor.

11. International processing

Billy, Discord, and service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers. Local privacy rights remain available where the law provides them.

12. Website cookies

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Essential hosting security and delivery technology may process basic request information. If analytics or optional cookies are added later, this Policy and any required consent controls will be updated first.

13. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may change as Billy adds features or legal requirements change. The updated date will be shown above, and material changes may also be announced on the website or in the official Discord.

14. Contact and deletion requests

To ask a privacy question or request access or deletion, contact the Billy team through the official Billy Discord support server. Include your Discord user ID, the relevant server ID, and a clear description of the request. Do not post private information in a public channel.
