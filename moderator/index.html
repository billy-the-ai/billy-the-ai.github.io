What a Billy Moderator actually does

Answer the hard ones Pick up the questions Billy could not answer, so the next person who asks gets a real answer instead of a shrug.
Teach Billy your corner Feed him what your community actually knows — the fixes, the rules, the things everyone repeats ten times a week.
Catch what slips through Flag bad calls: a warning that was wrong, a scam that got past, an answer that read badly. Those reports are what makes him better.
Test before release Try new features first and say when something is not ready. A moderator saying "this is confusing" stops it reaching 27 servers.

How applying works

  1. Fill in the form. A few questions about your experience and the community you help run. It takes about five minutes.
  2. Billy files it. Your application goes straight to the team's review channel and is checked against your Discord account.
  3. A person reads it. Not the bot. Someone on the team decides, and the decision is recorded with a reason.
  4. You hear back either way. Including if the answer is no, and why.

Being straight about this: moderator places are limited and most applications are declined, usually because the timing is wrong rather than the person. Applying does not affect how Billy treats you or your server in any way, and you can apply again later.
