Billy in Action

What Billy does

One Bot, Every Job

The main things. There's a lot more in the command list.

Answers questions

Billy learns from the channels your admins approve and answers from them. For general questions he can search the web, and he says when he isn't sure.

Moderation

Warnings, timeouts, bans, reports and logs. Billy can gather the evidence, but your staff always make the call.

Tickets and appeals

Private support tickets, punishment appeals and transcripts, so problems reach the right staff.

Levels and activity

Message and voice levels, daily streaks and leaderboards. He counts time in voice but never records it.

Forms and applications

Connect a Google Form and applications show up in a private staff channel with the member's activity attached.

Protection, always free

Anti-raid, lockdown, and honeypot channels that catch bot accounts. Safety is never behind a paywall.

Good to Know

Straight answers to what people ask most

  • Billy only learns from channels your admins approve. Private channels stay private unless someone picks them.
  • Billy never records voice chat. He only counts how long people spend in voice, for levels.
  • Each server's knowledge is kept apart. Billy never answers one server with another server's information.
  • Anti-raid, honeypot traps and lockdown are free on every plan, forever.
  • Typing / in Discord shows 28 entries, and together they hold all 278 of Billy's commands.
