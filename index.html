Questions

Before you invite Billy

Does Billy know every server automatically?

No. He only learns from channels a server admin approves. Private channels stay out unless someone picks them on purpose.

Does Billy replace human moderators?

No. He can organize evidence, send alerts and explain options, but punishments are always decided by your staff.

Does Billy record voice chat?

No. He counts how long people spend in voice channels for levels. He never records audio.

How do Helper Points work?

Nobody has to press anything. When a member asks a question and someone at or above the level your admins chose answers it with real help (a channel, a link or steps), Billy notices. A few seconds later he posts a picture of that conversation, saying who helped whom, for staff to approve (or gives the point automatically, if your server prefers). Each real help posts straight away, and staff approve it, so points can't be farmed.

Are the Marketing and Scouting Teams only for Billy?

No. Any server with Billy can run its own, with its own team role, staff channel, Apply button and points. Start with /marketing setup or /scout setup.

How does paying work?

Plans are per server, per month. You pay on our payment provider's own checkout page (card, PayPal, Google Pay or Apple Pay), so this site never sees your card. Cancel any time and the server goes back to Free without losing anything.
