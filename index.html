What Billy does

The main things. There's a lot more in the command list.

Answers questions

Billy learns from the channels your admins approve and answers from them. For general questions he can search the web, and he tells you when he isn't sure.

Moderation with a person in charge

Warnings, timeouts, bans, reports and logs. Billy can gather the evidence, but your staff always make the call.

Tickets and appeals

Private support tickets, punishment appeals and transcripts, so problems reach the right staff.

Levels and activity

Message and voice levels, daily streaks and leaderboards. He counts time in voice but never records it.

Forms and applications

Connect a Google Form and applications show up in a private staff channel with the member's activity attached.

Protection, always free

Anti-raid, lockdown, and honeypot channels that catch bot accounts. Safety is never behind a paywall.

Questions

Does Billy read every channel?

No. He only learns from channels a server admin approves. Private channels stay out unless someone picks them on purpose.

Does Billy replace moderators?

No. He can collect evidence, send alerts and explain options, but punishments are always decided by your staff.

Does Billy record voice chat?

No. He counts how long people spend in voice channels for levels. He never records audio.

Is one server's information shared with another?

No. Each server's knowledge is kept separate. The network checks on the Ultimate plan only ever share a count, never another server's name, its moderators or what they wrote.

How does paying work?

Plans are per server, per month. You pay on our payment provider's own checkout page (card, PayPal, Google Pay or Apple Pay), so this site never sees your card. Cancel any time and the server goes back to Free without losing anything.
