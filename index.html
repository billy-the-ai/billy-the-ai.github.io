Questions, answered

Before you invite Billy.

Does Billy know every server automatically?

Billy learns only from channels a server administrator approves. Private channels stay excluded unless they are intentionally configured for a private staff feature.

Does Billy replace human moderators?

No. Billy can organize evidence, send alerts, and explain options, but important moderation decisions should always stay with authorized staff.

Does Billy record voice conversations?

No. Billy can track time spent in voice channels for activity levels, but it does not record the audio.

How will Premium billing work?

Premium is planned as a monthly Discord app subscription. Discord will handle checkout and payment details; Billy will receive only the subscription entitlement needed to unlock features.
